Why most people stop posting on LinkedIn.

Growing on LinkedIn and building a personal brand takes consistency. These six problems get in the way.

The blank page

Three posts a week is a lot of writing. Generic AI tools make you sound like everyone else.

The silent first hour

A post that gets no early reactions is shown to very few people. You don't have a reliable circle that shows up.

Comment fatigue

Thoughtful comments on other people's posts bring profile visits, but writing fifteen a day takes an hour.

No idea what works

LinkedIn shows numbers, not answers. Which hooks, topics and times actually work for you?

Five tools, five bills

A post writer, a scheduler, an engagement pod and a comment extension, each with its own subscription.

The fear of getting flagged

LinkedIn automation tools and engagement pods put your account at risk. Your profile is too valuable to gamble with.

How Qiva Reach works.

Four steps from a blank page to real conversations. Set up once in about five minutes; after that, a week of posts takes one sitting.

  1. Teach it your voice

    Sign in with LinkedIn and paste a few of your past posts. Qiva Reach learns your tone, length and the phrases you never use.

  2. Draft and pick a hook

    Turn an idea, a voice note or a blog into a post. Choose from ten hooks and see exactly where "see more" will cut it off.

  3. Approve and schedule

    Edit anything, then schedule for the times your audience is active. It publishes through LinkedIn's official API.

  4. Your Circle shows up

    Members of your niche Circle get an alert and engage with your post themselves, by hand. You do the same for them.

A safe LinkedIn growth tool. No bots, no automation.

LinkedIn does not allow bots, auto-engagement or scraping, and accounts that use them get restricted. Qiva Reach is designed so that every action on LinkedIn is a click you make yourself.

What Qiva Reach does

  • Signs you in with LinkedIn's official login. We never see your password.
  • Publishes the posts you approved through LinkedIn's official sharing API.
  • Writes comment suggestions into the comment box. You press Post.
  • Sends your Circle an alert so real people choose to engage.

What it never does

  • Auto-like, auto-comment or auto-reshare anything.
  • Send connection requests or messages for you.
  • Scrape profiles, feeds or other people's data.
  • Run hidden browsers or store your LinkedIn session cookies.

What is Qiva Reach?

Qiva Reach is an AI LinkedIn post writer, scheduler and engagement community in one workspace. It drafts LinkedIn posts, hooks and comments in your own voice, publishes the posts you approve through LinkedIn's official API, and connects you with a small group of real people in your niche who engage with each other's posts.

Who is Qiva Reach for?

Founders, consultants, coaches, creators, B2B marketers and agencies who want to grow on LinkedIn and build a personal brand, without hiring a ghostwriter. It works anywhere LinkedIn does.

Is Qiva Reach a LinkedIn automation tool?

No. Qiva Reach never likes, comments, connects or messages on your behalf. It signs you in with LinkedIn's official login and publishes only the posts you have approved and scheduled. Every other action on LinkedIn is a click you make yourself.

Will using Qiva Reach get my LinkedIn account restricted?

Qiva Reach is designed to stay within LinkedIn's rules: official login, official publishing API, and no automated likes, comments, messages or scraping. No tool can promise LinkedIn's decisions, but we built this one to avoid the behaviour that gets accounts flagged.

What is a Circle? Is it an engagement pod?

A Circle is a small group of 20 to 50 professionals in a similar niche. When a member posts, the others get an alert and decide for themselves whether to react or comment. Unlike an engagement pod, nothing is automated, and a fairness score keeps it balanced.

Will the posts sound like AI?

They start from your own past posts, your tone settings and a list of phrases you never use. Every edit you make teaches it more. You stay the editor.

Can my agency use it for clients?

Yes. The Agency plan includes five client profiles, each with its own voice, plus approval links for clients and roles for your team.

When does Qiva Reach launch?

The private beta starts in December 2026 and the public launch is planned for January 2027. Waitlist members hear first, and the first 200 paying customers get 40% off yearly plans.

What happens to my data?

Your posts and voice profile are private to your workspace. You can export or delete your data at any time. We never sell data or use your posts to train models for anyone else.
